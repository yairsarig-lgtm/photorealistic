The Aura QR

Open the Aura camera on this phone

How it runs

  1. On their phone: open the camera, flash the QR past it, tap the link. Allow the camera the first time.
  2. The first screen shows the five symbols. Tap the one they chose; the camera opens with it on screen.
  3. Drag it where you want it with one finger. Pinch to resize. Tap the viewfinder to jump it there. Double-tap it (or the Glow chip) to switch Glow / Shadow; the % chip sets how strong it is.
  4. iPhone: press the shutter — the share sheet opens at once → tap Save Image. It's in Photos, as the newest photo. Swipe up to the home screen, open Photos, hand it over.
  5. Android: the shutter saves it straight away. It's in Gallery, in the Download album (Samsung: Albums → Download).

Nothing is needed on your own phone. Add ?debug=1 to the link to see the saved photo right after the shot while testing.
